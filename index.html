About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Industrial Engineering at Purdue University, advised by Dr. Young-Jun Son. My research sits at the intersection of human-AI interaction, human factors engineering and extended reality (XR).

My dissertation develops an adaptive, multimodal AI framework for mixed and virtual reality clinical training. The system combines conversational AI virtual patients with physiological signals, eye-tracking, behavior and dialogue to model trainees' stress, cognitive load and situational awareness, for individuals and for teams. More broadly, I study when and how intelligent systems should adapt to the people using them, with applications in healthcare, digital twins and autonomous systems.

My work has been published in venues including Educational Technology Research and Development and Sensors, and recognized with the Trailblazers in Engineering Fellowship and first place in the IISE Doctoral Colloquium 3-Minute Thesis competition.

Research

2022–present, dissertation research

Multimodal AI-enhanced XR framework for nursing training and evaluation

A mixed and virtual reality clinical training platform (Unity, C#, Python) in which nursing students interact with conversational AI virtual patients. The framework fuses physiological signals, eye-tracking, behavioral interactions and trainee dialogue to model stress, cognitive load and situational awareness in individual and team-based clinical scenarios.

2025–present

Embodied AI and digital twins for autonomous decision-making

Autonomous systems that turn incomplete, real-time perception into uncertainty-aware world models and adaptive decisions, including generative 3D scene completion for digital twin initialization under occlusion and context-aware dynamic risk modeling for mobile robot navigation.

2026–present, with Softpuzzle (South Korea)

Physics-informed fire AI foundation model for intelligent space safety

A graph neural network module within a two-stage CNN + PINN/GNN architecture that represents building spatial topology and evaluates evacuation-route graphs, supporting occupant risk analysis and dynamic evacuation guidance in an international R&D collaboration.
